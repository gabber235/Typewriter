Interdimensional Cooking

Tired of Earth food? Ready to expand your palate across dimensions? This guide covers everything from sourcing ingredients through reality rifts to proper etiquette when dining with eldritch beings.

Warning

Some recipes may cause temporary phase-shifting, existential revelation, or mild indigestion. Cook at your own risk.

Temporal Pasta

Section titled “Temporal Pasta”

A dish that cooks itself yesterday:

Cooking Instructions:

  1. Prepare ingredients
  2. Set temporal coordinates to T-24h
  3. The pasta is now already cooked
  4. You’re welcome

Experimental

Warning: Temporal pasta sometimes arrives before you cook it, creating a bootstrap paradox. Eat quickly.

With Non-Humanoid Beings

Section titled “With Non-Humanoid Beings”

Basic Rules:

  1. Don’t stare at eyes (any number of them)
  2. Compliment their aura, not their physical form
  3. Pass dishes through appropriate dimensional gaps
  4. Always offer the last bite (even if you can’t see them eating)
