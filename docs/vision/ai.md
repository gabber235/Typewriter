# AI

## What it is

AI assistance, planned for a milestone after the resource pack work. Not thought out yet. MCP is probably first, but this is far enough away that the order does not matter now.

Ideas:

- Generate voice lines from dialogue.
- Synchronize chat messages, action bar text, and scene dialogue with voice lines (generated or uploaded).
- Generate formulas from a text description.
- MCP access to Typewriter.
- An assistant in the panel: "create a quest that does this", producing all the needed entries.

## Who it serves

Content creators (faster creation), protagonists (voiced content).

## Why

Lower the effort to build rich content.
