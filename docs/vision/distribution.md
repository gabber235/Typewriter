# Distribution

## What it is

How Typewriter and extensions are updated and delivered. Everything, first party (extensions, engines, realms) and third party, is published through the [marketplace](marketplace.md).

- **Automatic updates by default.** No owner approval needed; owners can turn it off.
- **The publisher controls the rollout**: slowly (gradual, automatic) or all at once.
- **Feature flags**, also inside extensions. A publisher can sell an extension for free and make parts paid behind a flag.
- **Frequent updates** that do not break existing setups.
- Features that exist only on some Minecraft versions are capabilities (see [engines.md](engines.md)).

## Who it serves

Server owners (stable updates), extension developers (safe releases).

## Why

Update often without breaking servers.
