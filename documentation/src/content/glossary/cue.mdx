---
title: Cue
aliases: ["cue", "cues", "segment", "segments", "keyframe", "keyframes"]
---

A **cue** is a block on a [scene](/glossary/#scene)'s [track](/glossary/#track):
either a *segment* with a start and end frame, or a *keyframe* at a single
frame. Cues on one track cannot overlap. 20 frames make one second.
