---
title: Page element
aliases: ["page element", "page elements"]
---

A **page element** is anything placed on a [page](/glossary/#page): an
[entry](/glossary/#entry), a group of entries, or, on a
[scene](/glossary/#scene) page, a [cue](/glossary/#cue). Elements are the nodes
you see on the graph and the tracks you see on the timeline.
