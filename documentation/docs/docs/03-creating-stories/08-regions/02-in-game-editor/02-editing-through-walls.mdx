# Editing Through Walls

Blocks inside walls are hard to click. Switch to spectator mode (`F3 + N` or `/gamemode spectator`) without
leaving the editor: the tool you were holding stays active as your **ghost tool**, named in the boss bar.

A spectator client sends no left clicks, drops, or hotbar keys to the server, but right clicks do arrive. The
editor therefore gives right click the click duties and listens to the movement keys for everything else. On
the polygon wand, **right click** adds a corner at the block you occupy and **sneak right click** removes
the corner under your crosshair, no lock needed.

Fly to the spot and press **jump + sneak together** to lock in place. Your camera stays free, but the keys
now drive the tool instead of moving you:

- **Wand (two mark shapes)**: WASD, space and shift move the glowing selection cube block by block, straight
  through any wall; **ctrl** marks it, exactly like a left click. The marks fill primary then secondary, then
  replace whichever is closer.
- **Wand (polygon)**: aim at a corner and **WASD** nudge it half a block along the yaw cardinals, exactly
  like the resize tool drives faces. **Space** pins the aimed corner so the keys keep driving it while you
  look around; space again releases it.
- **Move**: WASD, space and shift nudge the whole region one block the way you face.
- **Rotate**: A/D turn the yaw; W tilts the region away from you, S pulls it back, in 15° steps, as a clean
  pitch or roll depending on which side you look from.
- **Resize**: aim at a face, W pushes it away from you, S pulls it toward you. **Space** pins the aimed face,
  so the keys keep driving it even when another face of the region slides in front of your view; space again
  releases it.

Press **jump + sneak** again to release and fly freely.
